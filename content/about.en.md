# About Me

I am an Electrical-Electronics Engineer with a deep-rooted passion for network infrastructure and information technology. Although my academic background is in electrical engineering, my fascination with networking and IT developed independently during my university years. 

Driven by this self-motivated interest, I focused on mastering routing, switching, and network security, which culminated in earning my **Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA)** certification.

Currently based in Germany, I am actively expanding my skill set into cloud technologies (Docker, Kubernetes, AWS) and cybersecurity. I believe that my hybrid background in both electrical engineering and network automation allows me to bring a unique, well-rounded engineering perspective to modern infrastructure challenges.

### Core Interests
* Enterprise Network Design & Implementation
* Network Security & Firewall Architectures
* Cloud Infrastructure & Automation
